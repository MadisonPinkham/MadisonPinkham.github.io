Projects

Temperature Sensor LCD System

Arduino-based system that reads body temperature and classifies it as cold, normal, or feverish.

Goal

Build a system that displays a person's temperature on an LCD and indicates if they are within a safe range, cold, or feverish.

Solution

  • Used an Arduino and breadboard to connect an LCD and an analog temperature sensor.
  • Leveraged the built-in ADC to convert analog readings to digital values.
  • Implemented logic to map temperature ranges to status labels on the display.

Results

Successfully created a compact prototype that provided real-time temperature feedback and status, demonstrating integration of hardware, firmware, and user-facing output.

Spend Sense - Financial Management IOS App

IOS financial management application that helps users track spending, analyze financial habits, simulate savings growth, and improve financial literacy through personalized insights.

Goal

Create an intuitive financial companion that helps students and young professionals understand their spending patterns, build better saving habits, and make informed decisions about their future finances.

Solution

  • Developed a native iOS application using SwiftUI and XCode with an interactive, user-focused interface.
  • Implemented a spending tracker to categorize purchases, visualize monthly spending trends, and identify unnecessary expenses.
  • Created a savings simulator allowing users to create portfolios, set monthly contributions, and estimate future growth based on projected returns.
  • Designed financial insight features including debt-to-income ratio analysis and personalized spending/savings recommendations.
  • Integrated financial education tools to help users improve budgeting, investing, and money management knowledge.

Results

Delivered a fully functional financial management application that combines expense tracking, savings forecasting, investment planning, and financial education into a single mobile experience. The app empowers users to better understand their finances and develop long-term financial habits.

GE Appliances – Scrap Reduction Project

Data-driven process improvement project to reduce side panel dents and cavity gaps in refrigerators.

Goal

Identify the root causes of recurring refrigeration unit damage and redesign the workflow to reduce scrap and rework on the post-foam case line.

Solution

  • Tracked damage locations and frequency in Excel to reveal patterns across the line.
  • Optimized movement of front-line workers and coached the repair team on a new action plan.
  • Added an additional foam pad checker to reduce missed units.

Results

Reduced scrap rates and improved overall reliability of refrigerator case production.
